# Asset provenance

This repository contains a new fictional identity and original visual material. It does not include the reference creator’s portrait, dog photographs, case-study screens, logos, product artwork, scripts, or downloaded website files.

## Original artwork

- **Product screens and diagrams:** original, hand-authored SVG interfaces for Verdant, Tally, Margin, Hearth, Soundcheck, Dispatch, and the supporting experiments.
- **Editorial illustrations and posters:** original SVG compositions, typography, grids, and diagrams made for this template.
- **Photography and collectible concepts:** generated specifically for this project, then edited, composited with the original interfaces, cropped, and converted into local WebP assets. These include the fictional designer portrait, workplace photography, prototype notebook photograph, scenic shop image, and Milo figurine concepts. They are not photographs of a verified real person or manufactured product.
- **Sample résumé:** original fictional text, provided as a replaceable two-page PDF. Its embedded font is DejaVu Sans.
- **README artwork:** local GIFs and screenshots made from this implementation. They do not depend on a third-party animation or badge service.
- **Iconography:** simple line icons, studio monogram, and Threadspace mark authored for this implementation.

Original SVGs, diagrams, and code are supplied with the repository under its MIT terms. You may reuse the supplied generated imagery with the template, but no exclusive rights or universal copyright eligibility are claimed for AI-generated material. Review relevant provider terms and applicable local law for your intended use. Replace the portrait and credentials when presenting this as your own personal portfolio.

## Fonts and packages

- **Geist Sans / Geist Mono:** bundled locally through the `geist` npm package; distributed under the SIL Open Font License. The package includes its upstream license. See [vercel/geist-font](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font).
- **DejaVu Sans:** embedded only in the sample PDF; distributed under the DejaVu/Bitstream font license. See the [DejaVu font license](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html).
